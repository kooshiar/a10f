03
The gap

LLMs can't do chemistry

  • Useful predictions need 1 kcal/mol accuracy. LLMs approximate.
  • The output is a force on every atom, in 3D, not text.
  • The same model must handle 2 atoms and 20,000 atoms.
04
Why now

Training data is now computed, not measured

  • Quantum simulation gives the exact answer without any lab work.
  • GPUs keep cutting the cost, so the data supply has no ceiling.
  • Meta has already published 200M+ labelled calculations, free to use.
